## Design Context

### Users
Impulse is for people doing sustained desk work on macOS, especially coding, debugging, note-taking, document handling, and multi-step AI-assisted workflows. They move between projects, sessions, files, screenshots, terminals, and browser tabs, and need the app to stay organized without feeling heavy.

### Brand Personality
Calm, capable, native. The product should feel like a serious desktop tool that is inspectable and trustworthy, not flashy, toy-like, or chat-first.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a macOS-native visual language with restrained contrast, subtle material depth, soft grouping, and clear hierarchy. Favor calm neutral surfaces, muted accents, concise labels, and interaction affordances that feel at home in a pro desktop app. Avoid loud gradients, glossy gimmicks, and overly branded UI.

### Design Principles
1. Keep the app feeling native to macOS first, customized second.
2. Surface structure clearly so project, session, and task context are readable at a glance.
3. Use restraint in color and motion; clarity matters more than expressiveness.
4. Make powerful features feel organized and inspectable rather than dense.
5. Preserve calm focus during long-running work sessions.
